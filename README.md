**Company:** Goodcabs

**Problem:** Goodcabs is a cab service company operating across tier-2 cities in India. Management wants to understand how the business is performing across cities, how passengers use the service, and where improvements are needed.

**Why is Data Analysis Needed:** My Goodcabs Performance Analysis project uses Power BI to understand trip performance, revenue, passenger behaviour, and customer satisfaction across different cities. I compared city-level performance to identify what is working well and where the company can improve. The goal was to turn data into simple, actionable insights that could help Goodcabs improve passenger experience, encourage repeat rides, and make better business decisions.
